The BPAY payment option will be unavailable from Friday 18 September 2026 to Saturday 26 September 2026.

Updating your Passport

Important: Please submit new passport details once only.

Important Payment Notice

If your card was charged but you did not receive a receipt with the Department of Home Affairs logo, this means the payment was authorised by your bank but not captured by the Department of Home Affairs. You will need to make the payment again.

Your card issuer will return the funds to your account in line with their reversal policy. This timeframe is outside the Department of Home Affairs’ control.

We apologise for any inconvenience caused.
